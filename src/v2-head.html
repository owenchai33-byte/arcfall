How to play

1. Set upOpen the game on a laptop or tablet and tap Host a game. Friends scan the QR code on the big screen with their phone camera and their phone becomes their controller. Up to 8 players: phones, people sharing the laptop, and bots. Pick Free for all or Red vs Blue.
2. Walk & aimUse the on-screen ◀ ▶ buttons (or ←→) to walk and ▲ ▼ (or ↑↓) to aim: one tap is 1°, hold to sweep. Walking uses the pink Energy bar.
3. FireHold FIRE (or Space): the Force bar fills at a steady speed, the same every time. Let go to shoot. The red triangle marks your last Force so you can repeat or nudge a shot.
Aim like a proThe gold ruler along the bottom and the number over each enemy are their distance. Sky shot: let go at Force 95 (the gold notch) with angle = 90 − distance. Wind: add the green number when it blows your way, take off the red one when it blows against you (2° per 1 wind). Or open the Force chart (C), pick an angle and read the Force for that distance.
Skill shotsShoot high: angles of 60° or more add up to +40% damage (SKY STRIKE). A direct hit is a CRITICAL (x1.5). Long shots add +20%. Stack them, like a high lob straight onto someone's head, for DOUBLE DAMAGE (x2).
4. ItemsUp to two per turn: x2 Shots, x3 Spread, +50% Power, Heal, Teleport and Shield. Keys 123. Grab falling supply boxes for bonuses, but some are traps!
5. SuperHitting and getting hit fills the Super ring. When full, tap it (or E) and your next shot is your hero's signature attack.
6. Turn orderHeavy weapons and items add delay, so the lightest player goes next. Knock rivals into the sea for a quick win.
Gold, levels & the shopEvery battle earns gold and XP: more for wins, damage, knockouts and skill shots, plus 150 for your first win each day. Spend gold in the Shop on outfits, wings, auras, shell trails, explosions and name plates. It's all looks, so nobody can buy a win. Phones keep their own gold.
7. Have fun with friendsDirect hits are CRITICAL. Blasts knock people back, so push rivals off the edge into the sea. Tease your friends with emotes (the 😀 button, keys 4-9, or on your phone).
